## What the schema says, and what it does not

```
pronouns  String  "Member-supplied, e.g. \"she/her\". Never derived from `gender`."
```

Probed, not assumed:

- **Free text.** `"they/them"` writes and reads back cleanly.
- **No length cap whatsoever.** A 300-character value was accepted in full.
  Whatever bound exists has to come from the app, so the field caps at 40 —
  long enough for `she/her, they/them`, nowhere near long enough for prose.
- **`""` is stored as null.** That is the clear, and it is how a member takes
  pronouns back off their profile.

## Decisions

### A list, plus a way out of the list

The design draws `Pronouns / Select ▾`. Taken literally that is a fixed
dropdown, which would be quick to build and would fail exactly the people this
field exists for. So the list is short — `she/her`, `he/him`, `they/them`,
`she/they`, `he/they` — and ends with **Self-describe**, which opens a text box.

Deliberately short, too. A longer list still cannot be complete, and every extra
row makes Self-describe harder to find for the member who needs it.

First in the list is **Not specified**, which sends `""` and clears the field.
That is a real state, not a placeholder: it matches null exactly, so it
round-trips.

### An unrecognised stored value is a self-description, never "unknown"

Somebody typed it. It comes back in the text box exactly as they left it, rather
than being quietly discarded because it is not on our list.

### Never derived from gender, in either direction

The schema asks for this in the field's own description and it is right.
Choosing a gender does not fill in pronouns; choosing pronouns does not touch
gender. There is a test on each platform that says so, because this is the kind
of "helpful" that gets added later by someone who does not know why it was left
out.

## A real defect the work surfaced

I could not reach screen 10 on device at all, and finding out why exposed
something worse.

Tapping **"Set a security PIN to be fully ready ›"** on the readiness card
opened **phone entry** — step 1 of 5. The card names one thing and delivers
another, and the step it delivers is one a member with no signal cannot pass.

The cause is the phone step landing yesterday: `load()` sends every member with
an unverified phone to `.phone`, regardless of what they asked for. Before 08/09
existed the wizard opened at Details, so the mismatch was invisible.

Readiness rows now carry the step they name, honoured whenever the member
actually walks it — asking for a phone step a verified member does not have is
ignored rather than stranding them on it.

This was not in the brief for B2. It is a bug the B2 work uncovered, in code
this session shipped, and it blocks a member from finishing setup — so it is
fixed rather than filed.
