Documents — fields, icons, order

The native apps already render admin document types, fields, the three new fields (state_of_issue, gun_owner as dropdowns; health_conditions as text), their options (we handle both the JSON-string and array shapes), and both sortOrder levels — and we do not swallow a failed load as empty config. Icons — decided: we’re keeping the curated local glyphs. Admin icon changes won’t reflect in the native apps by design; if that becomes a requirement, it’s a follow-up on our side and would need real, sized images.

Family members · screen 33B

Included seats are addable without a seat price

Separate from the six — a bug we found and fixed while comparing 33B to member-client. The screen required a seatPriceId to add any sub-account, so a member with only included (free) spots saw “no seat price configured” and no Invite. Matching member-client, a price is required only for additional (beyond-included) seats; included ones send none. Fixed on both platforms — every open included spot now shows the Invite action.

Follow-up: additional / chargeable seats aren’t surfaced yet — our Entitlement has no maxAdditionalSeats / fee, and the seat-price resolution ties into your Finance work. See the confirm below.
